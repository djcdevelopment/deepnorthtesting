# Image Generation Prompts — OpenArt / Flux / Midjourney

These prompts provide high-impact visual banners, hero art, and atmospheric concept art corresponding to each infographic.

> [!TIP]
> **Prompting Best Practice**: AI image models often garble micro-typography. These prompts are tuned for **clean vector visual metaphors**, bold iconography, and dark cyberpunk-Viking aesthetics. You can overlay crisp numbers and typography directly in Figma, Canva, or Photoshop after generation.
